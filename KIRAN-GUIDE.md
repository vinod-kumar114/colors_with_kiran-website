# Your Website — A Simple Guide

Assalam-o-Alaikum Kiran 👋

This is your guide to running **Colors with Kiran**. There is no coding
anywhere in here. If you can use WhatsApp and Instagram, you can run this
website.

---

## 1. Signing in

Go to your website address and add `/admin` at the end.

For example: `colorswithkiran.com/admin`

| | |
|---|---|
| **Username** | `kiran` |
| **Password** | `mehndi123` |

### ⚠️ Change your password first

Anyone who knows the address could sign in with that password. Please change it
straight away:

1. Click **Settings** in the green menu on the left
2. Scroll to **Change password**
3. Type a new password twice (at least 8 letters)
4. Click **Change password** — you will be asked to sign in again

The yellow warning at the top of your dashboard will disappear once you do.

---

## 2. Adding a new Mehndi design

This is the thing you will do most often.

1. Click **Portfolio** in the left menu
2. Click the green **+ Add design** button
3. Tap the dotted box and choose a photo from your phone or computer
4. Fill in:
   - **Design name** — e.g. *Elegant Arabic Mehndi*
   - **Category** — e.g. *Arabic Mehndi*
   - **Show in** — *Portfolio* for your main page, *Gallery* for older work
   - **Price type** — *Starting from…*, *Fixed price*, or *Price on request*
   - **Price** — just the number, like `1500`. The website adds "Rs." for you.
   - **Short description** — a line or two about the design
5. Tick **Feature this design on the homepage** if you want it on the front page
6. Click **Save design**

It appears on your website immediately. No waiting, no publishing step.

> **Photos:** Upload the biggest, clearest photo you have. The website
> automatically shrinks it and makes it load fast. You do not need to resize
> anything yourself. Maximum size is 12 MB.

---

## 3. Moving designs between Portfolio and Gallery

Your **Portfolio** is your best, current work.
Your **Gallery** is your archive of older designs.

Next to every design there is a button that says **→ Gallery** or
**→ Portfolio**. One tap moves it. Nothing is lost — you can move it back
whenever you like.

---

## 4. Featuring designs on the homepage

On the **Portfolio** page, tap the **☆ Feature** button next to any design.
It turns green and says **★ Featured**.

Featured designs appear in the "Designs worth showing off" section on your
homepage. Tap it again to remove it from there.

---

## 5. Deleting a design (and getting it back)

Tap **Delete** next to a design. It asks you to confirm, then moves it to
**Trash**.

**Your design is not gone.** It is removed from the website, but it is sitting
safely in the Trash tab in the left menu. Tap **↩ Restore** any time to bring
it back exactly as it was.

Only from inside the Trash can you delete something forever, and it will ask
you twice. This is on purpose — so you can never lose a photo by mistake.

---

## 6. Categories

Click **Categories** in the left menu.

- **To add one:** type the name (and an emoji if you like) and click *Add category*
- **To rename one:** click *Rename*
- **To reorder:** use the ↑ and ↓ arrows — this changes the order visitors see
- **To delete one:** click *Delete*

> **Deleting a category never deletes your designs.** They just become
> "uncategorised" and you can put them into another category by editing them.

---

## 7. Updating prices

Click **Pricing** in the left menu.

Every price is a box you can type directly into. Change the number, then click
**Save** on that row.

Type prices exactly how you want visitors to see them:

- `Rs. 1,500`
- `Rs. 8,000+`
- `Rs. 2,000 onwards`

**To hide a price** without deleting it, tap the green **Visible** button —
it turns grey and says *Hidden*. The row stays saved, it just disappears from
your website.

At the bottom you can edit the notice that appears under the table:

> *"Prices are starting prices and may vary depending on design, complexity,
> and event requirements."*

---

## 8. Booking requests

Click **Bookings** in the left menu. A red number in the menu tells you how
many new ones are waiting.

Every enquiry shows the client's name, phone number, event type, date, location
and message. For each one you can:

- **WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp with their number and a greeting already typed
- **Call** — dials them
- **Mark booked** — once the booking is confirmed
- **Archive** — to tidy old enquiries out of the way

---

## 9. The announcement banner

That coloured strip across the top of your website. Perfect for:

- *"Eid Bookings Are Now Open!"*
- *"Chand Raat Slots Available"*
- *"Limited Weekend Appointments Available"*

Click **Homepage** in the left menu. Change the text, then click **Save
changes**. To switch it off completely, untick **Show the announcement
banner**.

## 10. Your photo, story and contact details

| What you want to change | Where to click |
|---|---|
| Your photo, your story, specialties, services | **About** |
| Phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email, address | **Contact Info** |
| Hero photo and headline, welcome text, banner | **Homepage** |
| Website name, tagline, Google description | **Settings** |

Change whatever you like, then click **Save changes** at the bottom of the box.

> **Important:** the **WhatsApp number** in *Contact Info* is what every
> WhatsApp button on your website uses. Write it with the country code, like
> `+92 300 1234567`.

Where a box says *"one per line"*, press Enter between each item:

```
Bridal mehndi (full hands, arms & feet)
Intricate jaali and mandala work
Modern Arabic & floral styles
```

## 11. Testimonials

Click **Testimonials** → **+ Add review**.

Add the client's name, what they said, the type of event, and a star rating.
You can add their photo too, but it is optional — if you leave it out, the
website shows a pretty circle with the first letter of their name.

Tap **Visible** to hide a review without deleting it.

---

## Things worth knowing

**Everything saves instantly.** There is no "publish" button. When you click
Save, your website has already changed. Open your website in another tab to
check.

**You can use your phone.** The dashboard works properly on a phone — the menu
scrolls sideways across the top.

**Nothing is deleted by accident.** Designs go to Trash. Hidden prices and
reviews stay saved. Deleting a category keeps your designs.

**Your photos are optimised automatically.** Upload the full-size photo from
your camera. The website shrinks it, converts it to a modern fast format, and
makes a small thumbnail for the grids — all by itself.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| "Your session expired" | Sign in again at `/admin` — normal after two weeks |
| A photo will not upload | Must be JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, under 12 MB |
| Changes not showing | Refresh the page (pull down on phone, Ctrl+R on computer) |
| Forgot your password | Ask whoever set up the website — it can be reset |
| Deleted something by mistake | Check the **Trash** tab — it is almost certainly there |
